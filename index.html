01

Executive Summary and Strategic Stance

The Turkish hospitality (HoReCa) and tourism sector is going through a comprehensive network infrastructure transformation driven by high guest expectations, growing connected-device density and the spread of cloud-based hotel automation systems. TP-Link Omada Hospitality Solutions sits at the center of this transformation with seamless Wi-Fi 7 coverage, PoE switching, high-density congress-area connectivity and centralized cloud management architecture.

Reviewing the 20 trade shows, congresses and field periods in the 2026 Q4 and 2027 calendar shows that not every event offers the same B2B return on investment (ROI). To use the event budget, field engineering capacity and content production as efficiently as possible, events are split into four tiers.

Tier 1

Main Booth + Live Demo + Stage Talk

Vertical specialty fairs where hotel IT managers, technical managers, system integrators and purchasing directors place hardware and infrastructure orders.

  • Hotel-Tech Antalya (Dec 2026 & Dec 2027)
Tier 2

Panel / Sponsorship + VIP Experience

Prestige summits attended by senior hotel investors, chain hotel general managers and MICE decision makers.

  • 16th International Resort Tourism Congress – AKTOB
  • Tourism Investment Forum – TIF
  • International Istanbul Tourism Fair – ITF
  • Antalya Tourism Fair – ATF
Tier 3

Regional Dealer / Integrator Joint Booth

Fairs focused on regional renovation projects or general tourism/agencies, but with a technology hall.

  • HOSTECH by TUSİD
  • ANFAŞ Hotel Equipment
  • HOTEL SHOW 2027 Bodrum
  • ELES Expo 2027
  • TTI Izmir · IZCOREX · EMITT · Travel Expo Ankara
Tier 4

B2B Visitor / Digital Content & Success Stories

Events aimed more at chefs, gastronomy professionals and online travel agencies (OTAs) than network-hardware decision makers, plus the field installation period.

  • Uzakrota Global Travel Summit
  • Antalya Culinary Forum · F-Summit · Gastromasa
  • ACE of M.I.C.E.
  • May 2027 Field Installation Period

03

Priority Participation Categories and Detailed Action Plan

3.1 Tier 1: Main Booth Direct hardware & B2B sales

Booth budget and live demo equipment (rack cabinet, PoE switches, ceiling/wall-plate EAPs, Omada Cloud Controller screen) are concentrated on the fair that targets hotel technical teams directly.

01

Hotel-Tech Antalya 2026 & 2027

10–12 Dec 2026 · 9–11 Dec 2027 · NEST, Belek

Specialty fair for hotel technical equipment: electrical, energy, HVAC, automation, fire systems, laundry and more. It targets hotel technical managers, low-current chiefs and IT managers directly, and December is when Antalya resorts approve next year’s infrastructure budget. Set up a technical booth and run field-engineering demos with system integrators.

3.2 Stage Talks, Panels and VIP Sponsorship Tier 2 · Senior decision makers

At these events, thought-leadership talks, panel speaking and VIP experience areas deliver far higher prestige and ROI than large hardware booths.

AKTOB · 16th International Resort Tourism Congress

25–26 Nov 2026 · Antalya

Having the TP-Link B2B executive as a speaker in the “AI and Smart Operations in Tourism” panel should be a strategic priority.

The Invisible Backbone of Smart Hotels: Maximizing Guest Satisfaction with AI-Powered Wi-Fi 7 and Centralized Cloud Network Management

Tourism Investment Forum · TIF

February 2027 · Istanbul

Organized by TTYD, this summit gathers new hotel investors and international chain hotel developers.

Future-Ready Network Infrastructure for New Hotel Investments: Multi-Site Cloud Management and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Advantage

ITF (Sep 2027) & ATF (Oct 2027)

Istanbul · Antalya

Both fairs have their own “Tourism Summit” and “Focus Stage” stages. Existing booth participation must be supported by 20-minute case-study presentations on these stages.

3.3 Events Recommended for Budget Optimization Tier 3 & Tier 4

Equipment and Regional Fairs

HOSTECH · ANFAŞ · HOTEL SHOW Bodrum · ELES Expo

A compact presence is enough: share a booth with a regional integrator or a POS / PMS software partner and pre-book meetings with hotel purchasing and technical managers.

Gastronomy-Focused Summits

Antalya Culinary Forum · F-Summit · Gastromasa

Their audience is mostly chefs, gastronomy writers, F&B managers and food suppliers. Instead of spending Omada booth budget here, redirecting the savings to Hotel-Tech Antalya, where hotel technical managers attend, will directly raise B2B lead quality.

General Tourism and Agency Fairs

Uzakrota · TTI Izmir · EMITT · Travel Expo Ankara

Most visitors are tour operators and travel agencies. Rather than opening a large hardware booth alone, set up a joint/compact B2B meeting area with the regional authorized Omada System Integrator (SI) or Hotel PMS Software Partner, and plan one-to-one meetings with hotel general managers through the fair’s appointment system.

Marketing, Content and Social Media Communication Strategy

4.1 Strategic Stance and Key Principles

We position trade show and congress participation not merely as physical booth traffic, but as a year-round engine for B2B trust building, technical education and case-study content.

“Educate and add value first, then convert.”

Business Objective

Position TP-Link Omada as the authority on “seamless guest experience and effortless cloud management” among hotel IT managers, technical managers and investors; increase pre-show appointment requests and post-show qualified B2B project forms (leads).

Content Pillars

1/3

Customer Education

Solving dead zones in hotel rooms, planning high-density Wi-Fi 7 for congress halls and PoE budget calculation.

1/3

Industry Transformation

A vision of AI, smart hotel automation and sustainable network infrastructure from the AKTOB and TIF summits.

1/3

Brand & Conversion

Live Wi-Fi 7 (EAP783) demos at the HOSTECH, ANFAŞ and Hotel-Tech booths, and May 2027 field installation success stories.

Channel Fit

LinkedIn

Data-driven thought-leadership posts, panel summaries and trade show appointment calls for hotel GMs, IT directors and integrators.

Instagram & YouTube

Reels of the live “Smart Hotel Room” simulation at the booth, May 2027 field installation (before/after) documentaries and engineer interviews.

X (Twitter)

Live panel quotes and industry threads in real time from summits such as AKTOB, TIF and ACE of M.I.C.E.

05

Reputation Management, Risks and Cross-Functional Coordination

⚠ Brand Safety and Claim Verification

Speed, capacity and concurrent-user figures used in trade show presentations and social media copy must come only from verified, official product specifications.

Unreleased (roadmap) products must not appear in public booth and social media content; in internal meetings they should be labeled “internal, needs approval”.

To protect channel harmony and partner margins, no content may include pricing, discount rates or promotional wording that could create a MAP violation.

Success Metric (KPI) and Guardrail

Primary KPI Number of qualified B2B booth appointments booked through pre-show content, content save/share rate and number of verified hotel project leads collected at the booth.

Guardrail Technical support requests from existing hotel/corporate customers in social comments and DMs are answered within 30 minutes and routed to the Sr. Customer Support Manager and the technical team.

Cross-Functional Approval Flow

1

Sr. Event Manager & Sr. Marketing Manager

Finalize booth square meters, sponsorship budget (HOSTECH, AKTOB, Hotel-Tech, ANFAŞ) and approve the annual calendar.

2

Sr. B2B Product Manager & Sr. B2B GTM Manager

Confirm stock/launch status of the Wi-Fi 7 EAP models, PoE switches and hospitality hardware to be shown at the booth.

3

Sr. Digital Marketing Manager

Run LinkedIn Lead Gen ads 3 weeks before each fair, targeting “Hotel IT Manager”, “Hotel General Manager” and “Low-Current Project Manager” audiences in Antalya, Istanbul, Izmir and Muğla.
