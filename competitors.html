01

Four Groups of Players

Group 1

Network hardware makers & distributors

Direct competitors, active as sponsors or through integrators.

  • H3C, Maipu, Winncom
  • HPE Aruba, Ruckus (CommScope)
  • Huawei (eKit / GPON)
  • Ruijie / Reyee, Zyxel, Ubiquiti
Group 2

Regional system integrators (dealers / IT)

Global vendors are usually represented on their booths, not under their own logo.

  • Olbia Bilişim, F2 Connect
  • Art-In, AYZ, Lotis, Netko
  • Bimatek, GSG, Procom, Toptel
Group 3

Telecom operators & hotspot software

Managed Wi-Fi and fiber at congresses; guest login / logging software.

  • Türk Telekom, Turkcell
  • Mintyfi
Group 4

PMS, cloud POS & smart-room ecosystem

Not hardware rivals, but budget rivals and the best joint-booth partners.

  • SAN TSG, Paximum
  • Adisyo, Tumeva, Qure, Bilnex

04

Dealers, IT Integrators and Ecosystem Partners

Regional system integrators (SI)

Antalya · Istanbul · Izmir

They are not tied to one brand. Whoever offers deal registration, margin, fast stock and co-marketing (MDF / co-branded booths) gets recommended to hotel IT managers. Contact them before the Nov/Dec 2026 fairs so they also show Omada at Hotel-Tech Antalya and ANFAŞ.

05

Omada Counter-Moves benefit-led, never naming rivals

Rivals with license fees

Message: “Protect your IT budget with a transparent cost structure and centralized cloud management.” Use it at TIF and AKTOB.

Rivals with complex setup

Message: “Bring multi-property hotels online in minutes from one screen with Zero-Touch Provisioning.”

Rivals with weak local support

Message: “Meet TP-Link Türkiye’s engineering team with strong local stock and service.” Show site-survey and fast RMA.

Guardrails: zero competitor mention in any public channel, no direct comparison tables, and no price, discount or partnership-damaging promotions (channel peace / MAP).
